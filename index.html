❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does visa processing take?+
Standard processing takes 5–10 business days. Our premium express service can process most visas in 24–72 hours.
What documents do I need?+
You need a valid passport, personal photo, national ID, and destination-specific documents.
How does FIB Bank payment work?+
After completing your visa application, you'll be shown the secure FIB Bank checkout. Pay via card (USD or IQD). Upon success, an invoice PDF is automatically generated and emailed to you.
Can I track my application status?+
Yes! After logging in and submitting, your application dashboard shows real-time status: Pending → Under Review → Approved / Rejected.
Do you offer group tour packages?+
Absolutely. We offer custom group tours for families, corporate teams, and private groups with special pricing.
